1. Definitions

  • Referrer: a HAVIT member who has accepted these Program Terms and linked an impact.com account to their HAVIT account.
  • Referred Friend: a person who uses a Referrer's Referral Link or Referral Code and then purchases a new HAVIT subscription. It does not matter whether the person is a new or existing HAVIT member.
  • Referral Code and Referral Link: the unique code and sharing link issued to a Referrer.
  • Reward: the cash amount paid to a Referrer under Section 4.
  • impact.com: the referral program platform operated by Impact Tech, Inc. (USA), which we use to calculate and pay Rewards.

2. Eligibility and Joining

2.1 Who Can Join

The Program is offered to HAVIT members whom we make eligible in the app. You must meet the eligibility requirements of the Terms of Use (including being at least 18 years old). You may not join, or receive Rewards, if you reside in, or ask to be paid to an account located in, a country or region subject to U.S. or Korean government sanctions, or where impact.com does not offer payouts.

2.2 How to Join

To join, you must (a) accept these Program Terms, (b) give the separate consent to the provision of your personal data to impact.com, (c) sign up for impact.com and verify your email address, and (d) enter that email address in the app to link your accounts.

2.3 One Linked Account

One impact.com account can be linked to only one HAVIT account. To change or remove the link, contact HAVIT support.

3. How a Referral Counts

3.1 Linking a Referred Friend

A person is linked to you when they use your Referral Link or enter your Referral Code in the app. Whether they are a new or existing HAVIT member does not matter.

3.2 Recognized Referral

A referral is recognized when the linked person purchases a new HAVIT subscription. The Reward for that referral is confirmed only if the subscription remains active for 30 days after the purchase (Section 5).

3.3 Limits

You cannot use your own Referral Code or Referral Link. A person who has an active HAVIT subscription when they use a Referral Link or Referral Code is not eligible to become a Referred Friend. Only one Reward is paid for each Referred Friend.

3.4 Tracking Records

Referrals are tracked through your Referral Code and Referral Link. If our or impact.com's records differ from yours, our and impact.com's records prevail.

4. Rewards

  • The Reward is 50% of the purchase amount of the Referred Friend's new subscription, paid in cash.
  • No Reward is paid for renewals or any later payments of that subscription. Referred Friends do not receive a Reward under the Program.
  • Rewards are calculated in U.S. dollars (USD). Amounts shown in the app are approximate conversions for reference and may differ from the amount paid.
  • Rewards are separate from in-app coins. They are not credited to your HAVIT account and cannot be used to pay for subscriptions or in-app items.

5. Holding Period, Refunds, and Chargebacks

  • Each Reward is held for 30 days from the Referred Friend's purchase date before it becomes available for payout.
  • The Referred Friend's subscription must remain active for those 30 days. If the purchase is refunded, cancelled, or charged back during the holding period, the Reward is cancelled.
  • A refund after the holding period does not affect a Reward that has already been confirmed.
  • If a Referred Friend later deletes their HAVIT account, Rewards for referrals already recognized are not affected.

6. Payouts

  • Rewards are paid to you by impact.com.
  • To be paid, you must register your personal details, payout account, and tax information directly in impact.com's "Tax & Payout" settings and pass impact.com's review. Until you do, Rewards stay on hold and are not paid.
  • Payouts are made on the schedule you choose in impact.com (on the 1st and 15th of each month, or when your balance reaches a set amount), once your balance is at least USD 10 or the equivalent in your payout currency.
  • You cannot change your payout currency yourself after you first register it.
  • You must keep your payout information accurate and up to date. Neither HAVIT nor impact.com is responsible for delays or losses caused by incorrect payout details.
  • HAVIT does not receive or store the payout information you register with impact.com.

7. Exchange Rates and Fees

  • The exchange rate applied to a Reward is the rate at the time of the Referred Friend's purchase.
  • Transfer processing fees and currency conversion costs are deducted from the Reward.
  • Fees charged by your receiving bank or any intermediary bank are your responsibility.
  • HAVIT does not compensate for differences caused by exchange-rate changes or fees.

8. Unpaid Rewards

Except as provided in Section 13, confirmed Rewards belong to you and are held in your impact.com account until withdrawn; they do not expire. If you do not register payout information, Rewards remain on hold. If no payout is made for a long period, impact.com may treat your account as dormant under its policies and deduct a dormant account fee from your balance.

9. Independent Status and Taxes

  • You take part in the Program as an independent third party, not as an employee, agent, or representative of HAVIT. Nothing in these Program Terms creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.
  • You are solely responsible for reporting and paying any income tax or other taxes and charges on your Rewards. impact.com may ask you to submit tax forms before paying you, and payouts are held until the required forms are submitted. If tax forms are needed, impact.com contacts you directly by email, and the HAVIT app also shows a reminder to submit them.

10. Promoting HAVIT Responsibly

  • When you share your Referral Code or Referral Link, you must make clear that you take part in a referral program and may earn a Reward if someone subscribes.
  • You must comply with all laws that apply to your promotion, including advertising, endorsement disclosure, and anti-spam rules.
  • You must describe HAVIT honestly. Do not exaggerate or misrepresent its features or results.
  • HAVIT is a wellness app, not a medical device. Do not claim that HAVIT diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any medical condition, or that it guarantees any weight-loss or health result.
  • If people ask how HAVIT handles their data, refer them to the HAVIT Privacy Policy.

11. Prohibited Conduct

You may not:

  • refer yourself or any account you own or control;
  • create fake accounts, or repeatedly use multiple accounts, the same device, or the same app store account to generate referrals;
  • send spam, make false or misleading statements, or use another person's information without permission;
  • send your Referral Code or Referral Link in bulk by text message, email, or messenger to people who have not agreed to receive it, or post it repeatedly on public boards or comment sections of other apps or services;
  • offer incentives in exchange for app ratings or reviews;
  • encourage sign-ups from people with no genuine interest in HAVIT; or
  • otherwise manipulate or abuse the Program.

We may review referral activity to check compliance. If we reasonably determine that you have broken these Program Terms, we may refuse or cancel the related Rewards and restrict or end your participation in the Program.

12. Content You Create

You keep ownership of posts, videos, reviews, and other content you publicly share to promote HAVIT under the Program ("Promotional Content"). You grant HAVIT a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to reproduce, share, and display your Promotional Content on our website, social media channels, and marketing materials. We will credit you where reasonably possible. You confirm that you have the right to grant this license and that your Promotional Content does not infringe anyone else's rights, including intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights.

13. Leaving the Program

  • To leave the Program while keeping your HAVIT membership, contact HAVIT support.

If you delete your HAVIT account, you lose all Rewards immediately.

  • From the moment your HAVIT account is deleted, you can no longer receive any Reward. This includes Rewards in the holding period, Rewards that are confirmed but not yet paid out, and Rewards for referrals that would otherwise be recognized later. These Rewards are forfeited and are not paid.
  • Your impact.com account is unlinked from HAVIT at the same time. After that, no Reward from the Program is paid to that impact.com account.
  • HAVIT automatically asks impact.com to delete the Program data it received from HAVIT. impact.com destroys that data, including copies held by its service providers, within 30 days of the request.
  • Your Referral Code and Referral Link stop working immediately.
  • Forfeited Rewards are not restored if you later create a new HAVIT account.

If you want to receive your remaining Rewards, request your payout in impact.com before deleting your HAVIT account.

14. impact.com Services

Signing up for impact.com, registering payout information, and receiving payouts are also subject to impact.com's own terms of service and privacy policy.

HAVIT is not liable for losses caused by impact.com's service outages, account reviews, or payout delays, except where HAVIT acted intentionally or with gross negligence.

15. Changes and Termination

  • We may change the Program, including reward rates, eligibility, and duration, or end the Program. We will give at least 30 days' notice via in-app notification, email, or website posting.
  • Rewards for referrals recognized before the Program ends are paid under these Program Terms.

16. Governing Law and Disputes

These Program Terms are governed by the same law, and disputes are resolved in the same way, as set out in Sections 17 and 18 of the Terms of Use.
